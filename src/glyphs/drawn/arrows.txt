// Arrows drawn by hand on a grid, cell by cell (glyphs/arrows.py, _pixels):
// pixelated, the heads of pairs on 100 came out a cell over and under the
// shaft, so ⇄ read as two crosses and ↹ as daggers.
//
// A glyph is a line "grid styles character", styles separated by commas,
// then its cells, top row first: "#" ink, "." air. It stands where the
// arrow pixelates, centred on it, so its shafts stay on the rows of the
// minus. The arrow's alt (ss01) is the same: on 100 their heads come out
// alike. Upright, arrows that are it turned or mirrored (⇆ ⇅ ⇵ of ⇄, ⇋ of
// ⇌) are its cells so. Italic's are drawn as they stand, each: slanted by
// rows of cells, the swept barbs stood upright, and pixelating left blots
// where they bridged. Lines starting with // are notes.
