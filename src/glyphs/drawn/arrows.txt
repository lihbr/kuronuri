// shaft, so ⇄ read as two crosses and ↹ as daggers; Bold's heads, heavier,
// filled in on 100, and met between the shafts of pairs on 70.
//
// A glyph is a line "grid styles character", styles separated by commas,
// then its cells, top row first: "#" ink, "." air. It stands where the
// arrow pixelates, centred on it, so its shafts stay on the rows of the
// minus. The arrow's alt (ss01) is the same: these come out alike as
// pixelated. Upright, arrows that are it turned or mirrored (⇆ ⇅ ⇵ of ⇄,
// ⇋ of ⇌, ← ↑ ↓ of →) are its cells so. Italic's are drawn as they stand,
// each: slanted by rows of cells, the swept barbs stood upright, and
// pixelating left blots where they bridged. Lines starting with // are
// notes.
